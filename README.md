# EnergiQ

## AI-Based Energy Consumption Intelligence & Optimization System

EnergiQ is an AI-powered energy intelligence platform designed to understand energy consumption patterns, predict future consumption, detect abnormal usage, identify potential energy wastage, and recommend practical actions to reduce energy consumption and cost.
